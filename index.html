A House In History

The Sun Yat Sen Museum, formerly known as the Sun Yat Sen Penang Base is a beautifully-preserved house museum in the multicultural George Town world heritage site. This historical house has a permanent exhibition on Sun Yat Sen's early revolutionary period in Penang when he planned a historic uprising in the China Revolution of 1911.
